# Changelog

## 0.3.5 (2026-11-11)

- **A2P 10DLC Compliance & Trust Center Staging Engine (`legends_gohighlevel/governance.py`, `legends_gohighlevel/agency.py`, `legends_gohighlevel/cli.py`):** Added `a2p-preflight` (auditing GHL Business Profile parity, NANP 555-placeholder phones, 301 redirect pipes, and live HTTPS DOM forms/checkboxes/privacy policies), `a2p-campaign-pack` (generating TCR-ready Campaign Registration JSON and Markdown bundles with `--overwrite` and Controlled Folder Access guards), `a2p-trust-center-status`, and preview-first `a2p-trust-center-stage` with synthetic RFC 2606 defaults and zero fallback entity residue.
- **DNS-Rebinding & HTTPS Pinning (`legends_gohighlevel/governance.py`, `legends_gohighlevel/social.py`):** Enforced strict `https://` URLs, DNS resolution (`socket.getaddrinfo`) rejecting loopback, RFC 1918 private, link-local, multicast, reserved, unspecified, and CGNAT (`100.64.0.0/10`) IP ranges, and IP-pinned TLS connections (`_PinnedHTTPSHandler`) in `_probe_public_url` and `GhlXBridge.download_media`.
- **CLI Secret Argv Hardening & Symlink/File Safety (`legends_gohighlevel/cli.py`, `legends_gohighlevel/api.py`, `legends_gohighlevel/social.py`):** Refused raw secret literals on `sys.argv` for `--pit`, `--workflow-capi-token`, and `--capi-access-token` (requiring `@<path>`, `--pit-file`/`--pit-stdin`, or environment variables), removed `--reveal-token` from `pit-create`, restricted `_MACOS_SYSTEM_SYMLINKS` to Darwin, enforced `_reject_symlink_chain` and `1 MB` size limits across all JSON/media/SQLite file paths, and required `--confirm-plan` on `x-bridge-sync --apply`.
- **SSH Command Hardening & Multi-Write Step Tracking (`legends_gohighlevel/api.py`, `legends_gohighlevel/agency.py`, `legends_gohighlevel/crm.py`):** Added `shlex.quote` and `--` option terminators in `extract_worker_token_via_ssh` and attached `completed_steps` context to multi-step mutation errors in `apply_location_rebrand_plan`, `apply_subaccount_rebrand_plan`, and `apply_a2p_trust_center_stage_plan`.

- **Internal Workflow Step Graph X-Ray & Bulk Step Rebrand (`agency-workflow-xray`, `workflow-steps-rebrand`, `snapshot-share-link`):** Added `agency-workflow-xray` (`GET /workflow/:locationId/:workflowId`) to inspect internal workflow action steps (`workflowData.templates`) for hardcoded email `from_name`/`from_email`/`subject`/`preHeader`, Meta CAPI `pixel_id`/masked `access_token`, webhook URLs, and source brand-term leaks; added preview-first `workflow-steps-rebrand` (`PUT /workflow/:locationId/:workflowId`) to bulk-rewrite workflow step attributes across all workflows in a sub-account; added `snapshot-share-link` (`POST /snapshots/share/v2/link`).
- **Atomic Rebranding Engines (`subaccount-rebrand`, `location-rebrand`, `subaccount-update`):** Added atomic sub-account rebranding for both Sub-Account Managers (`subaccount-rebrand` via `GHL_PIT`) and Agency Owners (`location-rebrand` via `BackendClient`, including `--include-workflow-steps`) across Business Profile, Custom Values, Trigger Links, Calendars, and internal Workflow Steps.
- **Multi-Location Sub-Account Profile Manager & Extended CRM / Commerce Operations (`legends_gohighlevel/api.py`, `legends_gohighlevel/crm.py`, `legends_gohighlevel/cli.py`):** Added `subaccount-profile-set`, `subaccount-profile-use`, `subaccount-profile-delete`, `custom-field-upsert`, `custom-field-delete`, `trigger-link-upsert`, `trigger-link-delete`, `calendar-update`, `calendar-events`, `appointment-create`, `conversation-messages`, `conversation-send`, `subaccount-users`, `contact-notes`, `contact-tasks`, `contact-task-upsert`, `medias`, `products`, `invoices`, `transactions`, and `blogs`.

  - **Layer 2 (User V1/V2 scopes, worker health, and `+hubpro` aliases):** Expanded `UNNERFED_V1_PERMISSIONS` to all 57 camelCase boolean keys returned by `GET /users/{id}`, verified 130/130 active Account Admin V2 scopes (`CRITICAL_ACCOUNT_ADMIN_V2_SCOPES`), and handled optional `AGENCY_ONLY_V2_SCOPES` on agency workers.
  - **Layer 3 (Reporting V2 Dashboard RBAC):** Audits both `defaultDashboardId` and all custom `sharedDashboards` per sub-account (`unlock-dashboards`).
  - **Layer 4 (Custom SMTP locks):** Audits and unlocks `blockAddSmtp` at both company and location levels (`smtp-status`, `smtp-unlock`).
  - **Layer 5 (4-store + 14-product SaaS Billing v2 rebilling):** Expanded `SAAS_BILLING_V2_KEYS` from 11 to all 14 metered usage products (adding `whatsApp`, `emailWarmup`, and `creatorStudio`) and added `rebilling-status` and preview-first `rebilling-sync` across `saas-billing-v2`, `update-twilio-rebilling`, `location.settings.saasSettings`, and `location-metadata`.

  - **Agent Studio (Voice AI & Conversation AI):** `voice-ai-agents`, PII-lean `voice-ai-calls`, and `conversation-ai-agents`.
  - **Custom Objects & Associations v2:** `objects`, `object-records`, and `associations`.
